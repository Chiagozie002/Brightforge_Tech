About Me

The developer behind BrightForge

A closer look at my journey, skills, and how I approach building for the web.

Biography

I'm Chiagozie Bright, a frontend developer with a strong interest in building interfaces that feel considered — not just visually, but in how they behave: how they load, respond, fail gracefully, and adapt across devices.

My development journey started with hands-on PHP and JavaScript projects, which grew into a focus on frontend architecture: structuring code so it's easy to hand off, extend, and eventually connect to a real backend.

Alongside client and personal projects, I head the Innovation and Technology unit within a student organization aligned with the UN Sustainable Development Goals — currently working on a weather hazard and flood early-warning platform for local communities.
